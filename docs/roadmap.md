- Detailed implementation checklists and evidence stay in [progress.md](progress.md).
- Starting work requires investigation and the applicable approval gate even when an outcome is listed as Current.
- Refresh the roadmap when priorities change or a workstream starts, ships, is rejected, is cancelled, or is parked.
- Do not invent delivery dates, effort estimates, or commitments without evidence and a user decision.
